# Quantum-Inspired-Feature-Enhancement-For-MRI-Image-Denoising-Using-GANS

Medical images play a crucial role in disease diagnosis and treatment planning, where image 
quality directly impacts clinical decisions. However, noise introduced during acquisition can 
degrade image quality, leading to loss of critical details and potential misdiagnosis. Image 
denoising remains a fundamental challenge in computer vision, and Generative Adversarial 
Networks (GANs) have emerged as powerful tools for restoring corrupted images. In this 
work, a novel quantum-inspired adversarial denoising architecture is proposed to generate 
high-quality denoised images from noisy inputs. The model is based on a Quantum Deep 
Convolutional Generative Adversarial Network (QDCGAN), where the conventional 
DCGAN architecture is enhanced with a quantum-inspired layer to improve feature 
representation and denoising capability. 
The proposed approach is evaluated on a Brain Tumour MRI dataset using the TensorFlow 
Quantum platform. Experimental results demonstrate that the QDCGAN model outperforms 
traditional denoising techniques in terms of both Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR) and 
Structural Similarity Index Measure (SSIM). Specifically, the model achieves  PSNR as 
28.74 db and SSIM as 0.898 which is better compared to existing methods. These results 
highlight the effectiveness of integrating quantum-inspired characteristics into deep learning 
frameworks, offering enhanced diagnostic image quality and meaningful benefits for 
medical practitioners and patients.
